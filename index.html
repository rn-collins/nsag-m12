Nervous-System-Aware Education Systems

12 questions  ·  6 dimensions  ·  8–10 minutes

Professional training programs in medicine, nursing, law, and pharmacy are designed around content coverage and accreditation compliance — not around the physiological and psychological conditions under which professionals actually form judgment. The chronic stress conditions endemic to these programs impair the precise cognitive outcomes they intend to produce.

Question 1 of 12

This assessment scores your organization across six dimensions of nervous-system-aware education systems. Each question uses a five-point scale. Your tier report is generated immediately after you submit your email — no waiting, no sales call required.

For: Medical schools · Nursing programs (BSN–DNP) · Law schools · Pharmacy programs · CME and CLE providers

Q1 of 12
Curriculum Stress Architecture

Has your program conducted a formal audit of cumulative assessment load across a single academic term — including total required hours, deadline clustering, and evaluation density?

Q2 of 12
Curriculum Stress Architecture

Does your curriculum explicitly protect time for integration, reflection, and consolidation — as distinct from content delivery and graded evaluation?

Q3 of 12
Psychological Safety Infrastructure

Does your program have a mechanism for learners to report environmental or relational safety concerns that is separate from academic grievance and disciplinary processes?

Q4 of 12
Psychological Safety Infrastructure

Have faculty in your program received training on the distinction between productive challenge — which promotes learning — and threat activation — which impairs it?

Q5 of 12
Trauma-Informed Pedagogy

Do faculty in your program receive training on how trauma history affects professional training — including hypervigilance, dissociation, and impaired judgment formation under perceived threat?

Q6 of 12
Trauma-Informed Pedagogy

Does your program have a formal policy for modifying academic requirements or clinical scheduling for learners who present with acute stress-related impairment?

Q7 of 12
Sleep & Physiological Readiness

Does your program formally acknowledge and design around the relationship between schedule design — shift length, call schedules, early start times — and cognitive performance in clinical or professional settings?

Q8 of 12
Sleep & Physiological Readiness

Has your program modified evaluation timing or scheduling based on evidence about sleep-dependent memory consolidation and its effect on high-stakes assessment performance?

Q9 of 12
Professional Identity Formation

Does your program have an explicit, structured curriculum component devoted to professional identity formation — separate from accreditation requirements or professionalism grades?

Q10 of 12
Professional Identity Formation

Are learners supported in developing an explicit professional self-concept — including values, scope, and identity under stress — before or concurrent with clinical or professional placement?

Q11 of 12
Technology Integration Standards

Does your program have a published, evidence-based standard for when and how AI tools may be used in learning, assessment, and clinical decision support — not just a policy permitting or prohibiting use?

Q12 of 12
Technology Integration Standards

Has your program assessed how AI tool use affects the formation of clinical or professional judgment — particularly in early-stage learners whose decision-making frameworks are still being constructed?
